# Private Federalism authoring conventions

Use the shared [Blake methods style guide](https://github.com/docxology/blake/blob/45171a099a6a658c52ab689501de646a28485653/docs/style-guide.md)
for general method boundaries, provenance and manuscript syntax. The link
binds current authoring conventions to the selected Blake 0.4.0 source. Earlier
rendered papers retain the style and producer identities recorded in their own
receipts. Consult [project status](PROJECT_STATUS.md) for the current qualification
and next work.
These private additions concern this argument and its institutional model.

## Persons, powers and warrants

Keep persons, poetic-state interpretations, national formations, offices and
claimed competences typed separately. Name who can make, contest or change a
decision. An association's representation does not substitute for a dissenting
member's or affected nonmember's standing. Distinct office identifiers establish
issuer separation; use *independence* only when the evidence establishes the
relevant appointments, budget and practical control.

Use *stipulated* or *hypothetical* for the constructed institutional procedures.
Use *satisfies the declared conditions* for their calculated conjunction. An empty
residual profile is no certification of legitimacy, non-domination, lawfulness or
empirical freedom. H12's retaliation and disputed forum competence must stay
visible beside the modeled result. Revision, suspension, advice, provision and
punishment name different powers or acts.

## Time and comparison

Hours are exact nonnegative integer event times from notice, with half-open
protection and gap intervals `[start,end)`. Filing at closing is allowed; a
response at the consequence instant acts first by assumption. Readiness means
completed prerequisites. Available and support-enabled hours are usable,
nonoverlapping time within the filing window, not measured treatment effects.
Preserve the named person, interval location and original window when displaying
a deficit. Equal total hours do not establish equal access.

Define comparison duties before introducing an order. Use *partial order on
residual profiles* and *preorder on procedures*. Say whether minimality concerns
set inclusion, an intervention catalog or cost. This comparison has no cost or
weighted score. Its requirement sets are obligations to discharge, not
independent administrative changes. Reassess after changing any procedure,
especially where filing activates protection. Do not call an incomparable pair
equivalent merely because one total is equal.

## Scholarly integration

Lead a subsection with the question it answers. Develop the close reading before
the institutional extension, then state where the proposed construction goes
beyond the source. Retain speaker, dramatic sequence, theological pressure and
copy/transcription distinctions. For Jerusalem 45 the question concerns where
remedial force falls; for 73 it concerns provision during unsettled belonging.
Neither passage supplies our filing rules or proves the comparison order.

Read a recorded source before adding a claim. Distinguish Pettit's assumptions
about appeals, Shachar's proposed mechanism and Tully's challenge to procedures
from this project's implementation. Cite printed pages and identify PDF offsets
in reading records. Selected rereading does not upgrade complete-work credit.
Do not add duplicate quotation records for Q23 or Q38; refer back to them.

## Syntax, figures and delivery

Use named fenced formalism blocks, native automatic counters and `[@def:...]`,
`[@prop:...]`, `[@eq:...]`, `[@fig:...]` references. Formal propositions state
fixed inputs, domains and assumptions, then prove only the represented relation.
Normative targets must be named as commitments rather than conclusions of an
order calculation. Extend notation where symbols are introduced; keep local
review notation separate from corpus and intervention notation.

Captions specify counting unit, denominator or fixed family, source epoch,
comparison direction, exact interval convention and the interpretation ceiling.
New comparison captions are generated from the report through manuscript
variables. Do not paste numerical outputs into an independently edited caption.
Verify text, references, quotations, vector labels and final physical page layout
as distinct checks. Preparation and static HTML checks do not clear browser QA.
Preserve old sources and reports; publish new analyses into new output epochs.

## Pinned PDF reference style

The native PDF currently uses BibTeX plainnat. Represent web essays as `@misc`
with their existing publisher in `howpublished`; retain URLs, access dates and
reading locators. For journals identified by issue without a volume, retain the
issue in `note`. Do not invent a volume to satisfy a formatting warning. Keep the
machine-readable citation catalog and bibliography consistent. These serialization
choices do not change source identity or add reading credit.
