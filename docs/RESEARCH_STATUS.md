# Research status

This is an initial private research program and executable descriptive prototype, version 0.3.0. It is not a completed journal article, validated political theory, current legal opinion, or proof of Blake's metaphysics.

The central hypothesis is composition without capture: the federation contains STATES in the proposed Blakean sense, while NATIONS are temporal productions, combinations and generations that may cross those states. Several nations may coexist without becoming a single nation. Containment preserves intelligibility, encounter and passage; it grants no automatic sovereign seat to a state such as Rahab or Satan.

The attachments' historical catalog snapshots are 223 works/202 texts in the scope and 224/203 in the addendum, each with 21 relevant entries (nine core and twelve related). The current imported live index is reported separately. Differences in totals are dated source observations, not grounds for rewriting earlier reading histories.

The scope's nine core complete-extraction reads and three related complete short-work reads are inherited reports. The remaining nine related entries are selected-context reports. Importing or searching any text adds no reading credit. Scholarly claims, empirical findings, formal results inside stipulated assumptions, normative commitments, software checks and rights warrants remain separate.

Next scholarly gates include copy-specific collation, full critical reading of consequential counterarguments, version comparisons before portfolio errata, and specialist criticism of the institutional model. The project does not modify or republish predecessor papers.
