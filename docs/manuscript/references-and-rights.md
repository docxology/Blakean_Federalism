Complete main-text reading means the available extracted argument, not every cited work, an empirical replication or collation with the published version. PDF page numbers below are one-based. ## Rights and reproduction note

All four selected images are identified historical Blake impressions from the Library of Congress, not modern facsimile book pages. Their item-attached advisory reports no known U.S. copyright or other restrictions in the collection documents, with qualifications concerning other jurisdictions and users' responsibility to determine status. This is a no-known-restrictions advisory, not a CC0 dedication. Credit: Library of Congress, Rare Book and Special Collections Division; Lessing J. Rosenwald Collection.

Milton: [item 48031331](https://www.loc.gov/item/48031331/#rights-and-access), copy D, numbered leaf 35, 1804 [i.e. 1815?]. Jerusalem: [item 48038103](https://www.loc.gov/item/48038103/#rights-and-access), copy I, plates 52, 54 and 73, 1804 [i.e. 1832?], posthumous impressions. The four provider JPEGs are retained uncropped and unaltered; page layout scales them proportionally. Raw restricted corpus resources remain private and ignored. Selected reproduction does not clear any other image.

The user requested fair-use images. These historical selections provide a more direct item-specific public-domain/no-known-restrictions basis, without needing a fair-use fallback. Their bounded critical use nevertheless concerns the pictured text-image relations, not decorative substitution for the source. A full plate is necessary to examine the relation of marginal text, central figures and lettering; the manuscript supplies criticism and typeset quotation alongside it. The private research PDF is not a replacement for a high-resolution collection or modern edition. These considerations do not guarantee a fair-use result for other materials. [U.S. Copyright Office, four-factor guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html).

The four graphical abstracts are new project-authored vector schematics, not recovered Blake diagrams, empirical results or evidence of a four-organ constitution. Body typography is 10 point with 12.8 point leading, page margins 39.6 point (13.97 mm). Embedded Bitstream Vera fonts retain their license alongside the generated artifact. The final atlas reproduces each complete selected plate at a larger scale; a full scholarly collation of all copies remains future work.
