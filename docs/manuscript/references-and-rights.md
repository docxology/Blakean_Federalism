# References, reading boundaries and image rights

## Selected bibliography used in the argument

Bibliographic identity is distinct from reading depth. Complete main-text reading means the available extracted argument, not every cited work, an empirical replication or collation with the published version. PDF page numbers below are one-based. The separate annotated bibliography retains earlier leads and historical reading reports; this appendix records the new pass without silently upgrading unread books.
