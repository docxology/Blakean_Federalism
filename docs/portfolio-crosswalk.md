## Targeted correction docket

1. Before Pragmatism's supplemental America text is an excerpt with local 152-line numbering. It cannot support a completeness claim or be cited as the poem's complete line numbering.
2. Thirteen Angels and thirteen Governors are different groups in the primary poem. America 11:13–27 and 12:1–10 identify Boston's Angel's refusal and the Angels' response; the Governors appear separately at 13:1–5.
3. Architecture's attempted attribution of that refusal to Orc must itself be corrected against the primary text. This session checked the university-hosted electronic poem, but did not compare every predecessor version or publish errata.
4. Preserve Peircean realism and James's agreement-with-reality constraints. Social agreement alone must not become a truth criterion in the new model.
5. Before any predecessor revision, capture the exact old/new versions, locate each disputed claim, verify source context, retain the old version and obtain scholarly criticism. No predecessor project is changed by this repository.

Primary check: [America a Prophecy](https://blake.lib.asu.edu/html/america_a_prophecy.html). The current verification is of selected passages in an electronic edition, not every illuminated copy or plate image.
