# Selected facsimile observations, 0.2.0

These observations supplement rather than rewrite research/textual-discrepancies.json. The old ledger describes earlier source objects and remains unchanged.

- Milton, LOC copy D, scan/numbered leaf 35, corresponds by text to ASU M32. The small as multitudes addition is visible in the right margin beside the human-form/brotherhood passage. This supports its presence in this selected impression; its marginal position is not captured by a flat verse line. No all-copy variant collation is claimed.
- Jerusalem, LOC copy I, scan/plate 73, visibly reads howl in the Spectres of the Dead passage. This supports ASU's reading against the locally observed bowl transcription for that word and sheet. It does not justify editing protected recovered originals or generalizing about all editions.
- Jerusalem 52's right epigraph and left Rahab epigraph are separate spatial units. The displayed quotation reconstructs only the right epigraph. The surrounding anti-Deist prose remains interpretively relevant.
- Jerusalem 54 locates emanation in Great Eternity above a diagram labeled Reason, Pity, Wrath, This World and Desire. Its political application remains an argument, not a recovered constitutional schema.

All four complete sheets were visually inspected in this pass. Milton copy D is catalogued 1804 [i.e. 1815?], with an 1815 watermark. Jerusalem copy I is catalogued 1804 [i.e. 1832?], with 1831/1832 watermarks; these selected impressions are posthumous. Source IDs, exact download URLs and complete byte hashes are in research/manuscript-inputs.json. The original source objects remain private, unaltered and ignored.
