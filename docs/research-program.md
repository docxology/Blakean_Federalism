## Claims and their possible defeat

Textual claims face counterpassages, dramatic context and copy histories. Interpretive bridges face rival readings and conceptual equivocation. Normative commitments require reasons that do not depend on accepting Blake. Institutional claims face exclusion, capture, fiscal failure, inaccessible appeals and comparative cases. Formal claims hold only within declared assumptions. Software tests verify the implemented predicates and custody boundaries. Rights warrants require item-specific permissions where applicable.

Neither 59 source-integrity checks in the research pass nor a passing project suite settles literary interpretation or political legitimacy. The nine core and three related complete-extraction reads are reported in the supplied briefs; current-session reading credit is independently bounded. Indexing first, reading second remains an operating rule.
