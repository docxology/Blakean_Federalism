# Institutional design and proposed classification records

The executable record is this project's interpretive extension. Entomological Law does not already contain this schema. Entity, poetic-state interpretation, national affiliation, legal role, asserting institution, competence source and consequence are distinct. Issuing jurisdiction and level, reach, enforcement and limits occupy separate fields. Temporal scope and classification evidence do not substitute for legal authorization.

The prototype uses a stipulated-charter warrant for coercive powers in synthetic cases. This is a modeling requirement, not a theory that real authority arises only from a single written charter. Actual statutory, treaty, customary and constitutional sources require an expanded jurisdiction-specific model and legal review. No current California-law claim is made.

The fifteen rule codes inspect separate failure conditions: issuer type, jurisdiction type, subject collapse, identity capture, vision hierarchy, rights floor, forced exclusive register, missing public translation, coercion inferred from interpretation, missing authority details, missing contestation, merely formal exit, exclusion of affected nonmembers, unspecified resource burdens, and forced incorporation where treaty/refusal questions are implicated.

A passing record means those explicit predicates did not identify an omission. The truth of field values, quality of procedures, adequacy of material resources and legitimacy of the stipulated norms remain unverified. The software must not certify governments or people. There is no rank of national vision, composite freedom score or eternal-authority inference.

## Five case pairs

| Case | Bounded alternative | Capture alternative | Necessary further evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A Several nations in one territory | Common person-based services with overlapping cultural affiliation | Exclusive nationality register controls access | Language access, unaffiliated residents, internal dissent and effective service delivery |
| B One nation across jurisdictions | Limited cross-border cultural cooperation | Association claims all co-nationals everywhere | Agreements, mandates, reach, implementation capacity and responsibility for resident nonmembers |
| C A generation becomes a public | Revisable recognition without automatic coercive powers | Claimed eternal state authorizes taxation or police powers | Membership criteria, representation, competence grant, migration and revision |
| D Officials rank vision | Equal standing without a poetic eligibility test | Fourfold status controls office or resources | Actual selection rules, dissent penalties, public reasons and enforcement |
| E Land and water conflict | Explicit cost/risk allocation, nonmember standing and separate treaty/refusal inquiry | Wealthy autonomy exports burdens and erases prior relationships | Place-specific land history, hydrology, labor conditions, consent, fiscal incidence and enforceable remedies |

The case data are diagnostic alternatives, not estimated treatment effects or an exhaustive institutional comparison. A subsequent evaluation must include ordinary territorial, asymmetric multinational and hybrid arrangements under common material assumptions. A non-Blake baseline and three/four/six-lens ablations prevent terminology or a chosen number of lenses from doing covert normative work.

## A worked resource decision

Suppose a wealthy cultural association requests revenue autonomy while its water use burdens downstream residents and migrant workers. An issuer may be the shared federal forum; its legal competence still needs a separately stipulated source. Its issuing level does not determine the reach of its decision or the actor able to implement it. Relevant affected persons include workers and downstream residents who are not members of the association.

A bounded alternative assigns an enforceable duty to the water-service authority, creates funded nonmember standing, protects essential water access, and makes cost allocation and residual risk reviewable. Association affiliation does not excuse legitimate shared costs. An Indigenous people is not simply one more voluntary association: consent, treaty relationships and refusal require their own historical inquiry. The hypothetical record flags their omission; it cannot determine or replace them.

Revision must be possible without forcing a dissident to abandon home, language, family or culture. Exit and voice are distinct protections. A review forum named in a JSON record may still be inaccessible in practice; real effectiveness must be observed. The proposed translation obligation requires accessible reasons and explicit representation limits, rather than a claim that the issuer speaks for every affected person.

## Inspecting the checks in 0.3.0

Assessment schema 2 retains all fifteen checks as passed/failed rule records, alongside the failed findings. Conditional nonviolations are visible, and the workbench exposes the underlying decision inputs. Whitespace-only authority, representation, contestation, reach and resource fields do not count as supplied. Historical findings-only results remain archived, rather than gaining invented passing checks.

Voice, materially feasible exit and revision require distinct practical evidence. A stated appeal forum does not establish independence, affordable access or an effective remedy. Poetic-state and national-label ablation leaves these programmed assessments unchanged; this is not an empirical test of the labels' interpretive value.
